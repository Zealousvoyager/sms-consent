Sherrouse Properties respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect through this website and how we handle it. We keep it simple and collect as little as possible.

We don't sell, rent, or trade your information. We share it only with service providers that help run the site — such as our web host — or when required by law.

We keep information only as long as needed for the purpose it was collected, then remove it. We take reasonable steps to protect it. No method of transmission over the internet is fully secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security.
